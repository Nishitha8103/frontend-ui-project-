About Coastal Kitchen

Bringing authentic coastal flavours to food lovers since 2020.

Our Story

Coastal Kitchen was founded with a simple mission: to bring the rich and authentic flavours of coastal cuisine to every food lover.

Inspired by the traditions of Udupi, Mangalore, Kundapura and other coastal regions, we serve fresh seafood, biryanis, traditional meals, desserts and refreshing beverages.

Every dish is prepared using quality ingredients, traditional recipes and a passion for great food.

Mission

To deliver authentic coastal food experiences through quality ingredients, excellent service and customer satisfaction.

Vision

To become the most trusted and loved coastal food destination, known for taste, freshness and innovation.
